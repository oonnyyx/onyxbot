# Changelog

## 2026-11-18

- **Twitch support:** OnyxBot now works on Twitch channels too, with the same panel, games, widgets, alerts, points and moderation.
  - Streamers log in to the panel with their Twitch account.
  - Alerts for follows, subs, resubs, gifted subs, raids and Bits.
  - Games and widgets read Twitch chat live, with Twitch and 7TV emotes.
  - Automod works through Twitch (delete, timeout, ban).
  - Viewers log in to the reward shop with Twitch.
  - `!klip` creates a Twitch clip.

## 2026-11-16

- **Streamer panel:** the Overview tab is now channel analytics for the last 7, 30 or 90 days.

## 2026-11-14

- Public showcase of OnyxBot on GitHub (screenshots, GIFs, full feature list).
